## Mamba3 Kernel Stack Signal

Reference:

- https://www.together.ai/blog/mamba-3

Together's Mamba-3 writeup says the open kernels use a mix of Triton, TileLang,
and CuTe DSL. It specifically points to TileLang for MIMO prefill and CuTe DSL
for decode kernels. That supports keeping B200 MIMO training experiments in a
TileLang-first lane, with cuTile/CuTe DSL treated as a separate decode or
microkernel path rather than a direct Hopper patch generalization.
