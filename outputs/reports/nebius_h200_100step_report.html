Interpretation

The fixed FP8 tensorwise + MLP recompute profile now passes cleanly on a single Nebius H200 for 1024-token context at BS64, BS128 and BS192. The fix has two parts: Megatron TE checkpoint kwargs are bound into the forwarded callable, and FP8 tensorwise sweeps default to NVTE_DISABLE_NVRTC=1 to avoid the Transformer Engine RTC teardown crash seen with TE 2.16.0.

BS192 is the best current 1024-token smoke profile: 100 steps pass with no OOM, no skipped/nan iterations, no Kernel::~Kernel cleanup crash, about 122.9k tok/s over the last 20 iterations, and about 110.9GB nvidia-smi peak. Prior BS256 runs OOM near the H200 memory limit, so BS192 is the stable recommendation for the 10000-step trial.

For maximum MFU, the remaining performance branch is to rebuild the image with a newer Transformer Engine and retest --enable-nvrtc. On the current image, keeping NVRTC enabled is around 4% faster at BS64 but crashes during TE RTC cleanup after metrics are emitted.

Do not treat OK_TE_CLEANUP_SIGSEGV as a clean production status. It means 100 training steps and eval completed, then TE crashed during process teardown.
